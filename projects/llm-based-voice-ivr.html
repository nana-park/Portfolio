Strategy

Key product decisions made
for LLM-based Voice IVR.

EXPERIENCE TRANSFORMATION 01

From menu-based IVR
to natural AI conversation.

SIGNAL

Growing customer demand for AI-powered call experiences exposed the limitations of traditional menu-based IVRs.

DECISION

Introduced LLM-based voice routing that understands user intent and guides customers through natural conversations.

RESULT

Enabled businesses to offer AI-driven customer experiences without relying on complex menu structures.

ARCHITECTURE SHIFT 02

From monolithic chatbot flows
to composable conversation domains.

CONSTRAINT

Growing chatbot flows increased maintenance overhead, required full-scale QA for small changes, and created dependency on individual developers.

DECISION

Modularized IVR into independent conversation domains that could be dynamically connected through LLM-powered domain jump.

RESULT

Reduced IVR development lead time from 3 months to 2 weeks and enabled scalable service expansion.
