Strategy

Key product decisions made
while validating Hopzie as a creator-commerce system.

PIVOT 01

Reframed the product
under legal constraint.

Constraint

Media regulatory changes made the original direction difficult
to scale after funding was secured from Google.

Decision

Shifted Hopzie into creator-commerce infrastructure
for YouTube-based product discovery from online news creation.

Result

Reduced scope into creator-commerce workflows
with lower regulatory exposure within the same creation ecosystem,
validated through rapid interviews with 5 creators and viewers.

SCALE 02

Chose strategic archive
over platform dependency.

Signal

MVP traction was visible,
but platform-native commerce was rapidly expanding.

Insight

Platform-native commerce was shifting from distribution channel
to internal commerce infrastructure in YouTube product strategy.

Decision

Archived before sunk cost and dependency risk deepened.
